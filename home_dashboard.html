STRATEGIC RECOMMENDATIONS

💎 Reward Champions

Score 8–9: offer 2x loyalty points, early access to new titles, and exclusive membership tiers.

📈 Push Regular → Champions

Score 6–7: a "Rent 5, get 1 Free" campaign can nudge them into the Champions bracket.

⚠️ Reactivate At Risk

Score 4–5: time-limited "We Miss You" with 30% off to trigger a return visit before they drop to Low Value.
